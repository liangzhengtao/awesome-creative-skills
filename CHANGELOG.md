## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-17

### 🎉 Initial Release

#### 海报设计 (Poster Design)
- **Retro Movie Poster** — Transform photos into 1970s-80s movie poster art (Star Wars, Blade Runner, Pulp Fiction styles)
- **Vintage Travel Poster** — Create Art Deco and WPA-era travel posters (National Parks, Orient Express, TWA Airlines styles)
- **Music Album Cover** — Generate genre-specific album cover art (hip-hop, electronic, jazz, rock, classical)

#### 摄影后期 (Photo Post-processing)
- **Film Emulation** — Emulate analog film stocks (Kodak Portra, Fuji Velvia, Ilford HP5, Polaroid, Daguerreotype)
- **Cinematic Grade** — Apply Hollywood color grading (Teal & Orange, Bleach Bypass, Blade Runner 2049, Mad Max, The Matrix, Amélie)
- **Light Painting** — Create long exposure and light painting effects (city trails, star trails, steel wool, neon portraits)

#### 品牌设计 (Brand Design)
- **Logo Generation** — Generate logos across styles (minimal, geometric, wordmark, pictorial, abstract)
- **Brand Identity** — Create complete brand identity systems (minimalist, playful, luxury, eco-friendly)

#### 社交媒体 (Social Media)
- **Content Creator Pack** — Multi-platform social media content (Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest)

#### 插画创作 (Illustration)
- **Character Design** — Character and avatar design (anime, cartoon, pixel art, realistic, chibi, mascot, VTuber)
